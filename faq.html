Frequently Asked Questions 🥤

1. How do I place an order?

Simply navigate to our Products page, choose your favourite drinks, click "Add to Cart 🛒", and proceed to checkout by filling in your delivery details!

2. What payment methods do you accept?

We make payments easy and local! You can pay using MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, or choose Cash on Delivery when your drinks arrive.

3. Where do you deliver?

We currently deliver right across Kampala, Uganda, ensuring your drinks arrive fresh, cold, and fizzy!

4. Can I modify or cancel my order?

Since orders are processed quickly to keep your drinks ice-cold, please reach out to us immediately via our Contact Us page or phone if you need any adjustments.
